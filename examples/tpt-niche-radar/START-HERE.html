Install or update

  1. Extract the ZIP to a permanent folder on your computer.
  2. Updating your old unpacked extension? Replace the files inside its existing TPT Extension folder with the files in this package. Keep the same folder path. Open chrome://extensions and click its reload button. Your old scrapedData is migrated automatically if available.
  3. First installation? Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, select Load unpacked, and choose the extracted TPT Extension folder containing manifest.json.
  4. Pin Niche Radar in your toolbar. Click it, then Open research workspace.
  5. Click Load your 2 attached CSVs or drop your own files onto the large import area. Review the preview and click Import keywords.

Your supplied files contain 153 rows → 145 unique keywords, including eight overlapping rows. The included files have no observation dates, so the dashboard will not invent historical trends. They load only when you choose to import them.

Find the right opportunities

  • Creator opportunities: separates likely seller/program names and freebie searches from general product keywords. Choose All search intents to inspect everything.
  • Three ranking lenses: Balanced, Low competition, or Demand first.
  • Useful filters: TPT opportunity, minimum searches, resource upper bound, score, source, dates, and research status.
  • Quick views: low competition, long-tail, growing, and seasonal wording.
  • Inspect a keyword: see the score breakdown, evidence history, source files, research links, and intent override.
  • Star, compare, and plan: save opportunities, select up to five for comparison, and keep notes, tags, and progress.

Make older exports useful

  • Drop multiple CSVs together. UTF-8 CSV and tab/semicolon delimiters are supported.
  • Nonstandard headers? Use Column mapping in the import preview.
  • Exact duplicates merge. Conflicting metrics remain in the evidence history.
  • In Import history, assign the actual observation date and search window, e.g. “last 30 days.”
  • Newest dated evidence wins. Undated uploads cannot silently replace dated snapshots.
  • Trends require different observation dates and the same non-empty search window.
  • Re-importing identical files is safe. Entire duplicate imports are skipped.

Capture a live TPT analytics table

  1. Sign in to TPT and open a table with Keywords, Searches, and Resources column headers.
  2. Open the extension popup. Optionally enable Collect multiple pages, set the page limit (up to 50), and enter the search window shown by TPT.
  3. Click Capture visible analytics. Keep the TPT tab open. A floating panel shows progress and a stop button. You can close the extension popup.
  4. Open the research workspace, click Review captures, confirm the date/window, and import.

Each page is checkpointed. Repeated pages, stalled pagination, malformed rows, and missing headers are handled explicitly. Interrupted captures with saved rows become recoverable after 60 seconds. A capture reads only rendered rows; it cannot bypass sign-in or reveal data the site does not show. Pagination across full page reloads may interrupt collection; import the saved portion and continue from the next page.

The capture logic was tested against controlled analytics-table fixtures, including multi-page capture and cancellation. It has not been verified in your signed-in TPT account. If TPT changes its table layout, export a CSV and use the importer.

Click Back up workspace regularly. Restore a JSON backup through the same importer. Restore merges imports and research notes; newer timestamped notes win, while the current scoring preference stays unchanged.

Everything stays in your browser profile. Export a backup before uninstalling, moving the extension folder, switching profiles, or clearing browser data. No external service, subscription, or API key is required.

Understand the score

The score prioritizes research; it does not predict sales or prove a niche is profitable. It combines log-scaled demand, resource competition, search-to-resource balance, and the source’s TPT label. Bounded resource ranges use their upper bound; open ranges receive no competition or balance points. Missing resource values stay visible and unscored.
